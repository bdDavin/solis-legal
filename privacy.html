Solis Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

1. Who we are

Solis is developed and published by Ben Davin, Sweden. Contact: hello@trysolis.app.

2. What we collect

No account, no advertising, no tracking, and no profiling. Solis runs no servers of its own. The single exception is your purchase: to unlock Solis and keep it unlocked across reinstalls and devices, purchase state is processed by RevenueCat (see section 5). Your locations, searches, and camera never leave your device, and nothing is shared with third-party AI systems.

3. Permissions and on-device data

With your permission via iOS prompts, Solis uses your camera (for the AR overlay; never recorded or transmitted) and your location while in use (to calculate the sun's path). Your preferences and recent searches are stored only on your device. Lawful basis under GDPR: performance of the service you requested (Art 6(1)(b)) and your iOS-level consent (Art 6(1)(a)).

4. Retention and deletion

Everything except your purchase record stays on your device and is deleted when you uninstall Solis. Purchase records are retained by RevenueCat and Apple for as long as needed to honour your access; to have yours erased, contact hello@trysolis.app with your Support ID (Settings › Purchase).

5. Purchases

Payment is handled entirely by Apple; we never see your card, name, or address. To validate your purchase and restore it on your other devices, Solis sends a pseudonymous app user ID and your App Store purchase receipt to RevenueCat, Inc., acting as our processor under their privacy policy. This is not used for advertising or cross-app tracking. Lawful basis under GDPR: performance of our contract with you (Art 6(1)(b)).

6. Apple frameworks

Map tiles, reverse-geocoding, ARKit, Core Location, and StoreKit purchase queries are handled by Apple under Apple's own privacy terms.

7. Your rights

EU/EEA and UK users have the rights of information, access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability, objection, and freedom from solely automated decisions. Because Solis stores everything but your purchase record on-device, deleting the app exercises erasure for that data. For your purchase record, or any other request, contact hello@trysolis.app.

8. Complaints

You may complain to Sweden's IMY, your local EU/EEA data protection authority, or the UK's ICO.

9. Children

Solis is suitable for all ages. We collect no data from any user beyond the purchase record described above, and none of it is used for advertising or profiling.
